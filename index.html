The $15-an-hour expert thesis

AI should not only replace work. It can help a smart, early-career worker take on more valuable work—with guardrails and expert backup.

From novice worker to expert-guided work

A specialist workflow,
made usable by a junior caseworker.

ExpertOS does not declare a novice to be an expert. It packages the process, checks, and escalation paths that let a less-experienced worker contribute safely while experts focus on the judgment calls that need them.

FOR WORKERS

A path into higher-value work

Give smart entry-level staff structured support to take on more complex benefits and grant cases while learning the workflow.

FOR EXPERTS

FOR OPERATIONS

Expand capacity without lowering the bar

Standardize the process around rules, evidence, QA, and accountable approvals. Actual productivity and cost impact must be validated in deployment.

Capability with accountability

AI can widen who does the work.
Experts still set the bar.

ExpertOS separates what software can support—checking, structuring, comparing, and guiding—from the contextual judgment qualified people must own.

01

Evidence before inference

Every rule result shows the field, document, or source that supports it.

02

Uncertainty stays visible

Missing evidence routes to review. The system does not fabricate confidence.

03

Decisions stay human

AI outputs remain advisory. Named reviewers own material actions and reasoning.

04

History stays intact

Case actions, generated artifacts, status changes, and reviews form one audit trail.
